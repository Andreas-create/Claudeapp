Privacy

Brainbow does not collect personal information, does not use cookies, does not show ads, and has no accounts to sign into. There is no server that stores anything about you.

Last updated 28 July 2026

What stays on your device

Your progress — which levels you have reached, your stars, and which levels you lost — is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you played on. It is never uploaded, never synced between devices, and nobody else can read it. Clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it permanently. There is no way for us to recover it, because we never had a copy.

What is measured

Brainbow uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It is cookieless: it sets no identifiers, does not fingerprint your device, and does not follow you to other sites. It records a page view with the page address, referring site, country, browser and device type — never anything that identifies you personally, and never anything about how you played.

Because Brainbow works offline once installed, most play is never counted at all. The numbers are a rough measure of interest, nothing more. Cloudflare processes this data as our provider; their handling is described in Cloudflare's own privacy documentation.

What is never collected

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No location beyond the country Cloudflare infers from your connection.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • No data is sold, shared or used to build a profile of you.

Children

Brainbow is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your choices

To erase everything Brainbow has stored about you, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app. To avoid the analytics beacon entirely, most content blockers stop it, and it never fires when you are offline.
